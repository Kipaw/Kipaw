## About

I'm interested in the space where creativity meets technology --- where
thoughtful experiences are shaped by engineering.

With a background in Electrical Engineering, creative production, and
product development, I've explored different ways to turn ideas into
something tangible. Now, I'm focusing on **software engineering**:
building useful products, understanding the systems behind them, and
learning to write better software.

## Knowledge Before Creation

I believe good work begins with understanding. Every discipline has
foundations worth learning, and every skill deserves more than
surface-level familiarity.

That's why I value structured learning, professional certifications, and
hands-on practice --- not just to collect credentials, but to strengthen
my foundations and keep challenging what I know.
